title: Catastrophe Response Playbook
subtitle: Business Operations
---

## When this playbook applies

- A named storm or flood event is forecast to affect 500 or more policies
- Or the National Hurricane Center issues a hurricane warning for our territory
- Or Claims Operations declares a catastrophe code

## T-72 hours

- Pull an exposure report by ZIP code from the policy system
- Confirm independent adjusting firm capacity
- Pre-stage temporary claims staff and phone overflow
- Publish the "Before the Storm" policyholder message

## Landfall to T+3 days

- Open the catastrophe code in the claims system
- Switch FNOL lines to extended hours
- Daily 8:00 stand-up: claims, operations, communications, IT
- Track FNOL volume against the exposure estimate

## T+3 to T+30 days

- Move qualifying claims to desk adjusting to free field adjusters
- Offer advance payments where damage is confirmed by photo
- Weekly report to leadership: open claims, cycle time, payments

## Closing the event

- Close the catastrophe code when new FNOLs drop below 20 per day
- Hold a lessons-learned review within 30 days
- Update this playbook with what changed
