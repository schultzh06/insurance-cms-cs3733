# Purpose

These guidelines tell underwriters how to evaluate new and renewal residential
flood submissions written through the National Flood Insurance Program (NFIP)
Write Your Own arrangement, and when a submission must be referred. They
supplement, and never override, the NFIP Flood Insurance Manual.

# Eligibility

A building is eligible when all of the following are true:

- It is in a community that participates in the NFIP.
- It has two or more outside rigid walls and a fully secured roof.
- At least 51% of its actual cash value is above ground level.
- It is not located entirely in, on, or over water seaward of mean high tide.

Buildings in Coastal Barrier Resources System areas built after the
designation date are **not** eligible. Check Appendix E of the Flood
Insurance Manual before quoting any barrier-island risk.

# Information required at submission

| Item | Required for | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Signed application | All risks | Electronic signature accepted |
| Building occupancy and description | All risks | Single-family, 2–4 family, other residential |
| Foundation type | All risks | Slab, crawlspace, elevated on piers, basement |
| Elevation Certificate | Pre-FIRM elevated risks, optional otherwise | Required to apply an optional elevation rating |
| Prior loss history | All risks | Five years; NFIP and private claims |
| Replacement cost estimate | All risks | Used to validate Coverage A limit |

# Coverage limits

- **Coverage A (Building):** up to $250,000 for 1–4 family residences.
- **Coverage C (Contents):** up to $100,000 per unit.
- Coverage A must not exceed the building's replacement cost. If the requested
  limit is more than 110% of our replacement cost estimate, refer the file.

# Referral triggers

Refer to a senior underwriter before binding if **any** of these apply:

1. The property is on the Severe Repetitive Loss list (see Appendix F).
2. Two or more flood losses in the past ten years, regardless of amount.
3. The building has a basement with finished living space.
4. The first-floor height is below the base flood elevation.
5. The applicant requests a backdated effective date for any reason other than
   a loan closing.

# Waiting periods

Standard waiting period is 30 days. The exceptions we see most often:

- Loan closing — effective at closing when coverage is required by the lender.
- Map revision — 1-day waiting period within 13 months of a map change.
- Post-wildfire — no waiting period for qualifying properties.

# Documentation

Record your rationale in the underwriting notes field for every referral
decision. Auditors sample 5% of bound policies each quarter and look for a
clear reason on any exception.
