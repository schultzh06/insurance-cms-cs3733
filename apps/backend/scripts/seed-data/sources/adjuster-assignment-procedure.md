---
title: Adjuster Assignment Procedure
subtitle: Claims Operations standard procedure CO-114
---

# Scope

Applies to all residential flood claims from first notice of loss until an
adjuster accepts the assignment.

# Steps

1. **Verify coverage.** Confirm the policy was in force on the date of loss and
   note any open waiting period.
2. **Classify the claim.** Use the estimated loss from intake:
   - Under $25,000: desk adjuster.
   - $25,000 to $150,000: field adjuster.
   - Over $150,000, or any commercial building: large-loss unit.
3. **Check for a catastrophe event.** If the claims system shows an active
   catastrophe code for the ZIP code, follow the catastrophe response playbook
   instead of this procedure.
4. **Assign.** Choose the adjuster with the lowest open-file count in the
   territory who holds a current NFIP Flood Control Number.
5. **Notify.** The system emails the policyholder and agent. Call the
   policyholder within one business day if they flagged a vulnerable occupant.
6. **Diary.** Set a 3-day diary to confirm the adjuster has contacted the insured.

# Service standards

| Measure | Standard |
| --- | --- |
| Assignment after FNOL | 1 business day |
| Adjuster contact after assignment | 1 business day |
| Inspection after contact | 3 business days (non-catastrophe) |

# Reassignment

Reassign when the adjuster has not made contact within two business days or
declines the file. Record the reason in the file notes.
