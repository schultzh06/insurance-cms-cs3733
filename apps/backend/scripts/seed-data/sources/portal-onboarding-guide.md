# Content Portal — Onboarding Guide

Welcome! This guide covers what you need in your first week.

## Finding things

- **Search** understands plain language. Try "how do I cancel a policy" rather
  than guessing a file name.
- **Filters** narrow by persona, content type, status and tags.
- **Collections** group related documents. Favorite the ones your team uses.

## Working on documents

1. Open a document and choose **Check out** before editing. Others see it as
   locked until you check it back in.
2. Upload your new version and **Check in**. Owners are notified of the change.
3. Set an **expiration date** on anything time-sensitive, such as rate
   tables or bulletins. You'll get a reminder before it expires.

## Service requests

Use a service request when you need someone else to act: review a claim,
request an adjuster, or check a claim's status. Attach the relevant document
so the assignee has context.

## Getting help

Ask your team lead, or an admin, if you need a persona or permission changed.
