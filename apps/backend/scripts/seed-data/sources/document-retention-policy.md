# Document Retention Policy

**Owner:** Administration · **Applies to:** all employees and contractors

## Why we keep records

We keep records to pay claims correctly, meet regulatory and NFIP audit
requirements, and defend ourselves in litigation. We delete records when those
reasons end, because data we no longer need is a liability.

## Retention schedule

| Record type | Keep for | Starts from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Policy applications and declarations | 7 years | Policy expiration |
| Claim files, including photos | 7 years | Claim closure |
| Claim files in litigation | Until 2 years after final resolution | Resolution date |
| Underwriting notes and referrals | 7 years | Policy expiration |
| Actuarial work papers | 10 years | Filing date |
| Employee training records | 5 years | Training date |
| Marketing material | 3 years | Last use |

## Legal holds

When Legal issues a hold, affected records must not be deleted until the hold
is released, regardless of this schedule. Holds are listed on the Legal
intranet page.

## Where records live

The content management system is the system of record for documents. Copies
on laptops, shared drives or email are convenience copies and must not be the
only copy of anything on this schedule.
