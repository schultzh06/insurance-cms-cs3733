## Volume and cycle time

- 3,412 flood claims opened in Q3 (up 38% on Q2)
- Median FNOL to assignment: **2.6 days** (target 1.0)
- Median FNOL to first payment: **31 days** (target 21)
- 22% of claims reopened at least once

## Where the time goes

- 41% waiting for missing information from the policyholder
- 27% waiting for adjuster availability
- 18% internal handoffs between intake and assignment
- 14% everything else

## Pilot results: structured intake

- Two agencies used the new intake form for six weeks
- Missing-information requests fell from 1.9 to 0.6 per claim
- Assignment time fell to 0.8 days for pilot claims
- Agents asked for a status page above any other feature

## Recommendations

1. Roll the structured intake form out to all agencies in Q4
2. Auto-assign claims estimated under $25,000
3. Build the policyholder status page before the next hurricane season
4. Revisit reopen reasons with the claims oversight team
