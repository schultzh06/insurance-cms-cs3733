# Residential Flood Rate Indication — Summary

**Prepared by:** Actuarial Analysis
**Experience period:** accident years 2019–2025, evaluated as of September 30

## Result

The indicated overall rate change is **+7.8%**. We recommend filing **+6.5%**
and capping any individual policy change at +18%, consistent with the NFIP
glide path.

## Method

1. Paid and incurred loss triangles developed to ultimate with a volume-weighted
   chain ladder (see the loss development triangle workbook).
2. Ultimate losses trended to the proposed effective period at 4.5% per year.
3. Premium brought to current rate level using the parallelogram method.
4. Catastrophe load from the modeled average annual loss rather than
   the experience period, which contains two major hurricane years.

## Sensitivities

- Using the incurred triangle instead of paid lowers the indication to +6.9%.
- A 1-point change in the trend rate moves the indication by about 2.4 points.
- Excluding accident year 2021 (unusually low) raises the indication to +9.1%.

## Caveats

Development factors for the two most recent accident years rely heavily on
selected tail factors. The 2025 hurricane season was still open at the
evaluation date.
